# Arcade

Read before touching `app/[locale]/arcade/`, `components/arcade/`, `lib/arcade/`, the leaderboards or their Upstash store.

## Cabinets

The Arcade is the game library: one Cabinet per Hobby (`lib/arcade/cabinets.ts`), a cabinet `playable` or `soon`. The shelf's playable tiles open their cabinet, kept in the address as `?cabinet=<id>` (none for the first).

| Cabinet | Rules | Screen | The game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ollie | `lib/arcade/ollie.ts` | `components/arcade/ollie/` | a skate runner down a Paris street that gets faster and busier tier by tier: jump the furniture (a held press flies higher), kickflip, grind the benches, chain a combo |
| Surf | `lib/arcade/surf.ts` | `components/arcade/surf/` | Flappy Bird on a surfboard over a living swell: each press pops Lucas off the water (again in the air to climb) through the gap between a rock or buoy below and a jetty piling or gulls above, or he rides the low gates and the tubes on the water |
| Café | `lib/arcade/cafe.ts` | `components/arcade/cafe/` | a minesweeper on a café terrace: 9 by 9 upturned saucers, 10 knocked-over cups under them, the numbers in latte art, a sugar cube to mark a cup; the score is the clear time, the lowest best |
| Merge | `lib/arcade/merge.ts` | `components/arcade/merge/` | a 2048 in git beside Lucas's editor, for the Hobby coding: merging typo fixes into commits, commits into branches, up to 2048, v2.0, the release; the tiles wear the site's rarities, and the score is the classic one, the highest best |

Each game's rules are a plain, seeded module, tested beside it (`ollie.test.ts`, `surf.test.ts`): physics, collisions, scoring and the world laid out from the seed, advanced in fixed steps of 1/120s (`advance`, a frame counting for 0.25s at most), so it plays the same at any frame rate, with `steps`, the frame's `events`, and a replay from a seed and its inputs (`replayOllie`, `replaySurf`) for the leaderboard. What the games share is in `lib/arcade/run.ts` (the seeded generator, the stored best).

### Ollie's rules

- **Tiers.** A new tier every 30s (`TIER_TIME`), shown to the player as "level 2", "level 3"… (not Lucas's Level). The speed climbs from 340 to `MAX_SPEED` 950, reached after 51s; the clear street after each pattern shrinks with the run's time from about 1.9s to 0.8s (`gapTime`, ±15%, the shortest reached at 150s).
- **Patterns** (`PATTERNS`, `patternsAt`): a cone, a bin, a bench from tier 1; the double cone and low and high pigeons from tier 2; two cones one landing apart (`rejump`, a held jump cannot clear both) from 3; a bench then a low pigeon from 4; a high pigeon then a bin right after from 5. Fairness is tested, not assumed: `ollie.test.ts` searches every way of tapping, holding or waiting, and checks every pattern can be cleared at 340, 600 and 950, and any pattern after any other at top speed and the shortest gap. A new pattern or a shorter gap must pass the same search.
- **Tricks, one button.** A press on the ground jumps; in the air it starts a kickflip (`FLIP_TIME` 0.26s), unless he lands within `JUMP_BUFFER`, when it jumps again on landing; landing while the flip still turns is a crash. Letting go during the rise cuts a jump to a hop, so a full jump with a kickflip is hold, let go once past a hop's rise, press. Coming down on a bench's top grinds it (sparks, points per unit ridden) until he jumps off or rolls off its end, where a press within `COYOTE_TIME` still jumps; its side still crashes.
- **Scoring.** `score` is the distance (one point per 20 units, the smallest share) plus `bonus`: furniture cleared in a jump with a kickflip, a grind, or a high pigeon skated under scores at the multiplier and raises it (x1 to `MAX_COMBO` x5); furniture cleared by a plain jump scores at x1 and drops it back to x1; a kickflip over nothing scores at the multiplier without raising it; a sparkle is worth `sparkleValue` (the speed / 5, rounded to tens: 70 at the start, 190 at top speed) times the multiplier. Each frame's points are in `awards` for the screen's popups.

### Surf's rules

- **Tiers.** A new tier every 10 gates (`TIER_GATES`), shown as "level 2"…; the speed climbs from 200 to `MAX_SPEED` 400 (after 80s); the gap narrows from `START_GAP` 174 by 1.2 a gate to `MIN_GAP` 115 (`gapAt`); a flown gap's floor may move `maxShift` from the last one, 100 at tier 1 up to 200, and the sea between two gates grows with that move (`spacingFor`: `SHIFT_RATE` units of height a second).
- **The sea** is laid in sea units (`s`, the distance plus the world x): the swell rolls `SWELL.amp` high every `SWELL.length` units (`swellAt`), and he rides its surface when not flying; a set (`sets`) adds a `SET.height` wave that lifts him for free; a tube (`tubes`, `TUBE.seconds` long) puts the wave's lip `TUBE.lip` over the sea: riding inside scores `TUBE_RATE` a second at the multiplier, a pop hits the lip.
- **Patterns** (`PATTERN_TIER`, `patternsAt`, `layPattern`; the plain gate comes three times as often): a gate anywhere in reach and a gate at the very bottom to ride (`ride`, floor 0, nothing under it) from tier 1; one at the very top and a set lifting him over a rock from 2; diving gulls (`dive`: the ceiling starts `DIVE.rise` higher and is down once `DIVE.near` him, `ceilingOf`), a lone gull in the mid-sky and a tube from 3; top, bottom, top and a shark fin in the low water from 4. Buoys bob with the swell (`floorOf`). A lone gull or fin flies at him faster than the sea and reaches him `HAZARD_AFTER` seconds after its gate, and the next gate waits as long.
- **Fairness is tested:** `surf.test.ts` searches every way of pressing or not every 50ms (a player at 20 presses a second at most), with the dolphin's help not counting, and proves every pattern can be ridden at 200 and 400 from a low, a sea-level and a top gap, any pattern after any other at top speed with the narrowest gap, and the first 50 gates of real seeds; another test proves hovering mid-screen no longer gets through 20 gates.
- **Scoring.** `score` is 10 a gate plus `bonus`: a pass with his middle within `PERFECT_BAND` of the gap's (or riding, through a gate to ride) is perfect, 20 at the multiplier, which then goes up (x1 to `MAX_COMBO` x5), any other pass drops it to x1; a tube scores while ridden and raises it once out; a shell (`shellValue`, the speed / 4, rounded to tens: 50 to 100) scores at the multiplier. A rare dolphin (from tier 2) swims with him and forgives one hit (`shield`, then 0.8s untouchable). Each frame's points are in `awards` for the screen's popups.

A screen only feeds its rules the presses and frames, plays its events as the sound kit's cues and draws it on a canvas, in its own palette (DESIGN.md, the game screens); `ArcadeFloor` loads each screen with `next/dynamic` (`ssr: false`), so a game costs nothing to the other pages. The parts every cabinet shares are in `components/arcade/`: the marquee, the cards over the screen, the first-name strip, the deck and the how-to-play strip (`CabinetParts.tsx`, `HowToPlay` takes the game's rules, each with a touch wording where it differs), what the canvases share (`canvas-text.ts`: the level, the multiplier, the popups and the level's banner in the sticker style, and the blend of a game's palettes from one time of day to the next), and the runs, the leaderboard and the visitor's best in their `localStorage` (`use-board-run.ts`, `imjustlucas:arcade:<game>:best`).

Sounds come from the kit (`lib/sound/kit.ts`), through the global switch: `hop` for Ollie's jump and Surf's pop, `boop` for a kickflip starting or a perfect pass, `pickup` for a sparkle, a shell, a trick landed in style or a gate passed, `confirm` out of a tube, `toggleOn` for the dolphin and `toggleOff` when it saves him, `milestone` on each new tier, `miss` on the crash, `unlock` on a new best.

Adding a game: its rules module with its replay, its screen under `SCREENS` in `ArcadeFloor` (built from `CabinetParts` and `useBoardRun`), its entry in `CABINETS`, its texts under `Arcade.cabinets.<id>` in both dictionaries (`name`, `tagline`, `poseAlt`, `game`, `hint`, `hintTouch`, `started`, `empty`, and its `rules` for the how-to-play strip, with `level` for a game with tiers), and, for a leaderboard, its id in `LEADERBOARD_GAMES` (`lib/arcade/board.ts`) with its direction in `SCORE_ORDER`, its rules in `REPLAY` (`lib/arcade/leaderboard.ts`) and its name in the Admin's `GAME_NAMES`.

### Café's rules

Café is played turn by turn, not frame by frame (`cafe.test.ts`): an input is an action on a cell (`reveal`, `sugar`, `hint`) at the hundredth of a second it landed on (`STEP`), counted from the press on Play, and the clock starts at the first saucer turned.

- **A fair table.** Nothing is laid before the first saucer: `deal` lays the 10 cups from the seed and that saucer, never on it nor the eight around it (so the first turn always opens an area), and deals again from the seed until `solvable` clears the table without a guess. The solver plays as a careful player would, from what is on the table only: a number with all its cups found frees its other saucers, a number with as many saucers left as cups missing marks them, two numbers sharing saucers settle the ones only one of them sees, and the cups left settle the rest. A table needing a coin toss (a 50/50) is never kept. The same seed and first saucer lay the same table, so the server replays it; about one deal in ten is redone.
- **Turning.** A saucer with no cup around it opens its neighbours, and the steam spreads (`open` events carry each saucer's wave). A sugared saucer never turns, and stops the steam. Turning a number with as many sugars around it as its count turns its other neighbours (a wrong sugar spills a cup). Turning a cup ends the run (`lost`); turning the last safe saucer clears the table (`won`).
- **The petit café** (`hint`), once a run, turns the safe saucer nearest the given cell (the keyboard's or the last one pressed; one without sugar first) and adds `HINT_STEPS`, 10 seconds, to the time.
- **Score.** `score` is the clear time in hundredths, the petit café included; 0 for a run not cleared, which never ranks. `sugarsPlaced` counts the sugars put down, for the "no sugar" Trophy.
- **Replay.** `replayCafe` refuses inputs out of order, closer than `MIN_INPUT_GAP` (50ms, faster than a hand), past an hour, or on an unknown cell or action; the leaderboard only keeps a cleared table.
- **Sounds:** `press` for a saucer turned, `pickup` when the steam opens an area, `boop` for a sugar put down and `toggleOff` taken off, `toggleOn` for the petit café, `miss` on a spill, `milestone` on a cleared table (then `unlock` on a new best). Café's screen also has the how-to-play strip (`HowToPlay` in `CabinetParts.tsx`, its lines under `Arcade.cabinets.cafe.rules`) and the petit café's button in the deck (`Deck` takes the game's own buttons).

### Merge's rules

Merge is played turn by turn, like Café (`merge.test.ts`): an input is a move (`up`, `down`, `left`, `right`) at the hundredth of a second (`STEP`) it landed on, counted from the press on Play.

- **A move** (`slideBoard`) slides every tile as far as it goes; two of the same value meeting merge into one of twice the value, the pair nearest the edge first, a tile merging at most once a move (2 2 2 2 makes 4 4). A move that moves nothing is no move: nothing lands, it is not kept.
- **New tiles** come from the seed (`random` in `run.ts`, its state carried in the run): two at the start, then one after every move on an empty cell, a 2 or, one time in ten (`FOUR_CHANCE`), a 4. The same seed and moves make the same boards.
- **The end** comes when no move is left (`canMove`: no empty cell, no two equal neighbours). Reaching 2048 (`GOAL`) does not end the run: the first one is the `shipped` event, v2.0 shipped, and the run goes on.
- **Score.** `score` is the classic one, the sum of every merge's value.
- **Tiles' names and rarities** are the cabinet's (`components/arcade/merge/`, DESIGN.md): 2 typo fix, 4 commit, 8 branch, 16 PR, 32 review, 64 feature, 128 hotfix, 256 sprint, 512 milestone, 1024 RC, then the version a tile ships (2048 v2.0, 4096 v3.0); 2 to 8 common, to 128 rare, to 1024 epic, then legendary.
- **Replay.** `replayMerge` refuses moves out of order, closer than `MIN_INPUT_GAP` (50ms, faster than a hand; the cabinet drops a held key's repeats closer than that), past three hours (`MAX_RUN_STEPS`, as long as a run stays open), more than `MAX_MOVES` (20,000, far past a 32768 tile), not a move, a move that moves nothing, or a run not over. The board then holds only finished runs.
- **Sounds:** `tick` for a move with no merge, `boop` for a merge, `milestone` for a new top tile from 128, `unlock` for v2.0 shipped (and its Trophy), `miss` when no move is left (then `unlock` on a new best).

## Leaderboards

Each game has a top 10, every player's best kept once under the first name they chose (DESIGN.md, Arcade).

- **The player** (`components/arcade/use-player.ts`): an id made in the visitor's browser (`crypto.randomUUID`) and, once given, their first name, both in `localStorage` (`imjustlucas:arcade:player`). The name is asked the first time a run would make the board (`wouldRank` in `lib/arcade/board.ts`), then reused. It must pass `parseName` (`lib/arcade/player-name.ts`, plain, checked as typed and again on the server): 1 to 16 characters, letters of any script, digits, spaces, `'`, `.` and `-`, and not one of a short list of insults.
- **A run** is handed out by the server before it is played (`startRun`): a seed, kept in the store with the time it was handed out. The screen records every press (and, in Ollie, every release) of the button with the step it landed on (`OllieInput`, `SurfInput`, one shape), in Café every action on a cell with its hundredth (`CafeInput`), or in Merge every move with its hundredth (`MergeInput`), and a run good enough for the board is sent back as its run id, the player's id and name, and those inputs (`submitRun`; `RunInput` in `board.ts`).
- **Which way a score goes** (`SCORE_ORDER` in `board.ts`): points, the highest best (Ollie, Surf, Merge), or a time, the lowest best (Café, in hundredths). The store keeps a player's best with `ZADD GT` or `LT` and reads the board in that order, `wouldRank` and the visitor's own best (`useBest`, `bestOf`) follow it, and the board and the Admin show a time as `1:07.42` (`formatScore`). A time was kept as a time rather than turned into points so the store, the Admin and the replay all read the same number.
- **The score is never taken from the browser.** `submitRun` takes the run out of the store (once: a second send is `expired`), replays it from its seed and inputs with that game's own rules (`REPLAY`: `replayOllie`, `replaySurf`, `replayCafe` or `replayMerge`; Surf skips releases; inputs of another game's shape, presses sent to Café or Merge, cells or moves to Ollie, are refused), refuses inputs out of order, a run that never crashes, a table not cleared or a Merge board with a move left (`refused`), refuses a run sent faster than the time its steps take (`refused`), then keeps the replayed score if it beats the player's best. A bot can still play well, but it cannot claim a score its presses did not earn. Café's time is the steps its inputs claim: the seed is known before the first saucer, so a script could lay the table and send a fast clean run: inputs at least 50ms apart set a floor on the time, not on who played it. Merge is turn-based too, with no clock in its score: the same check applies to the hundredth its last move claims, so a run of n moves can only be sent once 50ms × (n − 1) have passed since it was handed out (the moves claim at least that much time, and the server checks the real time is longer). A script can still play well, but it cannot send a long run sooner than a hand could have made its moves; the moves themselves are replayed, so the score is always what they earned. A result carries `MAX_MOVES` inputs at most, within the Server Actions' 4.5mb body.
- **Limits**: each visitor (by IP, `x-forwarded-for`) may start 60 runs and send 20 results per 10 minutes (`LIMITS`); open runs expire after 3 hours.
- **Removing a player**: the Owner takes a name off a board from the Admin's Arcade page (`app/admin/arcade/`), which checks the Owner itself.
- **Changing a game's rules**: scores replayed under the old rules no longer compare (Ollie's and Surf's tiers and tricks, October 2026), so the pull request says the board must be cleared once merged, and the Owner removes its entries from the Admin's Arcade page.

The rules are Effect code (ADR-0002) in `lib/arcade/leaderboard.ts`, over the `Scores` service. Adapters: Upstash Redis through its REST API (`leaderboard-upstash.ts`, over `lib/upstash.ts`, the plain `fetch` pipeline the Lobby and the Pokes share; no client library) and in memory for tests (`leaderboard-in-memory.ts`; `leaderboard.test.ts` plays real runs against it with the test clock). `lib/arcade/server.ts` (server only) is the only place that runs them; it returns plain data from `lib/arcade/board.ts`, and when Upstash cannot be reached it logs and answers `unavailable`: the game still plays, the board says it is resting, nothing is sent. The public Server Actions are `app/[locale]/arcade/actions.ts`.

Upstash keys: `arcade:run:<id>` (an open run, 3 hours), `arcade:<game>:scores` (sorted set of player ids by best score, `ZADD GT`, or `LT` for a time), `arcade:<game>:names` (hash of player id to name), `arcade:limit:<key>:<window>` (rate limit counters). The same database holds the Lobby's `lobby:online` (docs/lobby.md) and the Pokes' `poke:…` keys (docs/poke.md), and all share the free plan's 500,000 commands a month.

## Environment variables

The store is Upstash for Redis from the Vercel Marketplace (`imjustlucas-arcade`, connected to every environment of the `imjustlucas-dev` project). Development uses the same database as Production, so test entries must be removed afterwards.

| Variable | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `KV_REST_API_URL`, `KV_REST_API_TOKEN` | Set by the integration (`vercel env pull` for `.env.local`); `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL` / `_TOKEN` are read too. Unset: the leaderboards are `unavailable` |
